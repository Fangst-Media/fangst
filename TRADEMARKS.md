# Name and logo

The code is open source, but the name **Fangst**, its logo and the `fangst-media` organisation belong to the maintainer, Allan Kimmer Jensen. The code licences do not give you the right to use them.

You may:

- Fork the code, change it and release your fork under the same licences, **with a different name and logo**.
- Say your fork or product is "based on Fangst", or that it works with Fangst.
- Use the name to talk about the project: reviews, articles, guides, videos, integrations.
- Publish unmodified Fangst packages and container images, for example in a NAS app store, as long as you don't present them as official.

You may not:

- Publish a fork or a modified build under the name Fangst, or a confusingly similar name, as a package, container image, app store entry or download.
- Present a fork, a hosted service or a product as official, or as made or endorsed by the maintainer.

When in doubt, open an issue and ask.
